ScrollSync Guide v1.1.0

Let the page move while your hands rest.

ScrollSync scrolls at a steady speed, clicks through galleries on a timer, or hands the page to a gamepad. Set it up once in the popup, then lean back.

Getting started

  • Power switchThe switch at the top right of the popup turns ScrollSync on for this tab only. Settings are remembered for the tab until you close it.
  • One mode at a timePick Scroll, Auto, or Pad. Turning a mode on switches the others off and turns ScrollSync on if it was off.
  • PauseThe large round button pauses and resumes. It sits pressed in while something is running and fills with colour when paused. Space does the same.
  • Set up while offEvery control works while ScrollSync is off, so you can prepare speed or timing before you start.
  • Hover for helpRest the pointer on any button in the popup to see a short note on what it does.
infoScrollSync can't run on browser pages such as chrome:// or the new-tab page. Reload a tab that was open before you installed it.

Auto-scroll

Moves the page at a steady speed. ScrollSync scrolls the page itself, or the largest scrolling panel on it. If nothing scrolls vertically it moves sideways instead, and it can drive smooth-scroll libraries that some sites use.

  • Speed0 to 500 pixels per second.
  • DirectionDown or Up. The arrow buttons beside Pause, and the A and D keys, flip it too.
touch_app

Auto Pulse

Clicks on a timer, made for image galleries, comic and manga readers, and carousels that turn the page when you click one side of the image.

  • TargetThe largest image or canvas visible on screen. If there is none, the click lands on the page itself.
  • Click pointLeft, Center, or Right, or any exact position from 0 to 100% across the image. A pink ring shows where the next click will land.
  • Interval1 to 60 seconds between clicks.
stadia_controller

Gamepad

Connect a controller and press any button once so the browser can see it. The further you tilt the stick, the faster the page moves.

  • Vertical / SidewaysChoose which stick directions scroll the page. They can be on together.
  • ClickA or the right trigger clicks the current image. Pushing the stick fully left or right clicks its left or right side.
  • StickUse the left or right stick.
  • Sensitivity1 to 50. Higher moves further for the same tilt.
radio_button_checked

On-page hub

A small round control that floats over the page. Turn it on with the hub button in the popup header, or right-click the page and choose Show Floating Controls.

  • ClickOpens the full controls beside the page.
  • Right-clickOpens quick actions: pause, power, and controls.
  • DragMoves it anywhere on screen.
  • Reading itPressed in with a pink icon while running, raised with an amber pause icon when paused, raised and grey when off.
keyboard

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcuts work while ScrollSync is on and you are not typing in a text field.

Space Pause or resume.
WS Scroll: speed up or slow down by 5 px/s. Auto Pulse: click 1 second sooner or later.
AD Scroll: scroll up or down. Auto Pulse: move the click point to the left (10%) or right (90%).
